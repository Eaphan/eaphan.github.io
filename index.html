Yifan Zhang

Yifan Zhang is currently an associate professor at Shanghai University. He earned his Ph.D. from City University of Hong Kong, where he was advised by Prof. Hou Junhui. Prior to his doctoral studies, he received his M.E. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2019 under the supervision of Prof. Chengliang Liu, and his B.S. degree from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2016. He used to work in industry and had some grounding experience with algorithms. His research interests include computer vision, machine learning and robotics. He is open to collaborating with other researchers who are interested in related fields.

His current research interests primarily focus on

  • Computer Vision: 3D Scene Understanding, Multi-modal Fusion, Event-based Vision.
  • Machine Learning: Self-supervised Learning, Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, Adversarial Robustness
  • Intelligent Robotics: Autonomous Driving Perception, Robotic Manipulation and Navigation
